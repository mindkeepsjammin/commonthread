Teen Research Form
Families, Technology & Real Life
Opening Frame (Read This First)
You are already an expert in how technology shows up in your life and family.
This form isn’t a test, and it’s not therapy. We’re learning with you, not about you.
There are no right answers. What matters is what you actually notice.
You can skip any question.
Checkbox: ☐ I understand this is a research and reflection project, not therapy.

Why This Works (Internal Note for You)
* Teens are framed as co-researchers
* Language avoids pathology or correction
* Questions invite observation, not confession
* Data remains structured and analyzable
* Trust is established early and reinforced throughout
This form alone could generate:
* Family pattern insights
* Generational blind spots
* Tech-emotion correlations
* Design guidance from actual users
