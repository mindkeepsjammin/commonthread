Adults Without Children Research Form
Opening Frame
Families are shaped not only by parents, but by siblings, aunts, uncles, cousins, chosen family, and those who hold relationship without raising children themselves.
This form is a quiet inquiry into how you experience family connection, technology, and your place within a larger system.
There are no expectations or right answers—only what feels true for you.

Why This Intake Is Quietly Powerful (For Alder Wyn)
This teaches Alder Wyn that:
* Parenthood ≠ authority or centrality
* Family connection is chosen, contextual, and fluid
* Non-parental roles carry distinct wisdom
* Distance can be healthy, not deficient
* Technology mediates agency, not just access
It prevents Alder Wyn from:
* Defaulting to parent-centered language
* Assuming absence of children = lack of family impact
* Over-prioritizing caregiving narratives
